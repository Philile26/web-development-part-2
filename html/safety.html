The "Front Seat" Mobile Phone Rule

Ensure the driver is focused solely on the road. Commuters seated in the front row are encouraged to politely remind operators that mobile phone utilization while operating a public service vehicle violates provincial transit regulations.

Overloading & Capacity Caps

Do not board an operational vehicle that exceeds its legal capacity limit (strictly capped at 15 passengers + 1 driver). Overloading compromises vehicular braking distance capabilities and voids commuter insurance allocations.

Emergency Transit Panic Hotline

For immediate field intervention, tracking assistance, or security service dispatch, immediately phone our centralized toll-free support helpline at 0800-TAXI-SAFE.
